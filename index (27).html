00 · Our Belief

"Indians don't camp because there's nowhere consistent to go — not because they don't want to."

Why We Exist

Every weekend, millions across Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi and Hyderabad crave a break from the concrete — but have nowhere reliable to go. The nearest hill station is a 6-hour drive. The gear doesn't pack itself.

Campvana.in changes this. We're building India's first branded camping chain — permanent, premium grounds within 3 hours of every major city. You arrive. We handle everything else.

03 · The Campvana.in Way

Four principles.
One standard.

— One

Consistent. Always.

Whether it's your first visit or your tenth, every Campvana.in ground delivers the same premium standard. Same tent quality, same F&B, same warmth. Consistency is our moat — and your peace of mind.

— Two

No Roughing It Required.

We believe nature and comfort are not opposites. You shouldn't have to choose between the wild and a good night's sleep. Every detail — from your pillow to your post-hike coffee — is thought of in advance.

— Three

Close Enough to Go Weekly.

We deliberately site every ground within a 3-hour drive. Not a once-a-year expedition — a weekly ritual. Friday evening out, Sunday afternoon back. Nature as a regular part of how you live.

— Four

Land as a Living Thing.

We choose sites that are ecologically meaningful — ridgelines, river bends, canopy edges. Zero permanent structures. We treat every site as a long-term relationship, not a transaction.

"Camping as entertainment. India's weekly ritual for every family, finally within reach."
